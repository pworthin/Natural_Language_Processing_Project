Apple Inc. CEO Tim Cook announced new artificial intelligence (AI) features 
for iPhones and iPads during a press conference at the company's headquarters in Cupertino, California, on January 15, 2025. 
The company pledged to invest $10 billion in AI research over the next five years, starting from March 2025.

Microsoft Corporation, headquartered in Redmond, Washington, responded by announcing a $7.5 billion acquisition 
of an AI startup based in Berlin, Germany. The deal is expected to close in Q4 2025 pending regulatory approval from the European Commission.

Meanwhile, Tesla's CEO Elon Musk met with German Chancellor Olaf Scholz in Berlin to discuss expanding the Gigafactory. 
The meeting took place just days before Musk's scheduled trip to Tokyo, Japan, where he is set to unveil the next-generation Tesla Roadster.

In the sports world, Lionel Messi signed a two-year contract with Paris Saint-Germain (PSG) worth €80 million, 
while Novak Djokovic won his 25th Grand Slam title at the Australian Open in Melbourne, Australia.

NASA announced plans to launch the Artemis III mission in November 2026, which will land astronauts on the Moon 
for the first time since 1972. The mission will be launched from Kennedy Space Center in Florida aboard the Space Launch System (SLS) rocket.

In the entertainment industry, Warner Bros. Pictures revealed that the sequel to "Dune" will premiere in Los Angeles, California, 
on December 18, 2025, with lead actors Timothée Chalamet and Zendaya returning to their roles.
